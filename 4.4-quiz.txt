Question #1
What would the range of a 5-bit signed integer be?

Answer: 1 bit for sign, and 4 for magnitude gives us a range of -2^4 to 2^4-1
or a range from -16 to 15


Question #2
a) What is the result of 13 / 5?

Answer: Since this is integer division, the result is 2


b) What is the result of -13 / 5?

Answer: Since this is integer division, the result is -2
