Question #1
For each of the following, indicate what output they produce:

a)
std::cout << 3 + 4 << '\n';

Answer: 7, followed by a new line


b)
std::cout << 3 + 4 - 5 << '\n';

Answer: 2, followed by a new line


c)
std::cout << 2 + 3 * 4 << '\n';

Answer: 14, followed by a new line


d) Extra credit:
int x { 2 };
std::cout << (x = 5) << '\n';

Answer: 5, followed by a new line
