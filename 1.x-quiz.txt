Question #1
What is the difference between initialization and assignment? How many times can a variable be initialized or assigned a value?

Answer: Initialization is the first value assignment that a variable receives, during or right after definition.
Assignment is when a value is assigned to a variable and may happen at any moment after the variable was defined and more than once.


Question #2
When does undefined behavior occur? What are the consequences of undefined behavior?

Answer: Undefined behavior happens when the program executes a statement where the expected behavior is not defined by the language standard and thus it's implementation dependent.
That means it's unrealiable as a different compiler may change results as running on a different platform. We can't rely on undefined behavior because it's inconsistent.
Running into undefined behavior means we can no longer be sure of what will happen when the program reaches that point and it may malfunction or not.
